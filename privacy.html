Privacy

KERF Workshop Quoter is a desktop program from Metalran. It runs on your computer. It does not create an online account and it does not send your quotes, customers, or company details to us.

Workshop data stays in local files on that PC, including any .kerf.json file you choose to save. A license key is checked on the computer. It is not sent to a server.

If you send feedback, it goes out only when you choose to send it, through your own email.

This download page is hosted on GitHub. We do not run a separate tracker here. GitHub may record ordinary download and page requests as the host.
